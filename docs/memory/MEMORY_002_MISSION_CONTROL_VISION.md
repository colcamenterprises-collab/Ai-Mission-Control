# MEMORY_002 — Mission Control Strategic Vision

## Memory type

Strategic founder vision / operating context.

## Core vision

Mission Control is the pivotal team and operating system that supports Cameron's current businesses and will eventually become a system provided to clients to help them scale.

Mission Control is the epicentre of Cameron's businesses, ideas, and execution. It is designed to become the autonomous team that can turn Cameron's visions into real-world outcomes with speed, structure, and accountability.

## Mission Control as corporate headquarters

Mission Control should be understood as the corporate headquarters.

Agents are the team working inside that headquarters. They should operate toward a shared vision, with defined roles, specialists, management layers, and an organisational structure designed for autonomous execution rather than never-ending decision-making.

The long-term goal is not only to have agents complete isolated tasks, but to build a coordinated AI workforce that can manage businesses, projects, opportunities, research, marketing, operations, and product development.

## Agent knowledge requirements

Agents must understand:

- their own specialist field
- how their role fits within the wider business
- the business they are currently supporting
- the shared group vision
- operational context across Customli, Smash Brothers Burgers, SBB Dashboard, and future ventures
- a knowledge bank informed by the greatest minds in each relevant field of expertise

Agents should combine expert-level role knowledge with deep business-specific context.

## Customli

Customli will become an AI marketing agency run and managed by skilled AI agents across sales, marketing, research, operations, and development.

Mission Control will sit under the Customli brand.

Mission Control will eventually become a tool that can be provided to customers so they can leverage the group's agent systems, knowledge, execution capability, and operating frameworks.

## Smash Brothers Burgers

Smash Brothers Burgers is currently a traditional burger restaurant, but it will increasingly use Mission Control for analysis, research, opportunity identification, marketing, operational improvement, and business scaling.

The food business is intended to become a multi-brand operation, including:

- Smash Brothers Burgers
- Shake N Fries
- Oh My Dog Hot Dogs
- Toasty with the Mostly — Toasted Sandwiches

The direction is toward a ghost kitchen operation rather than traditional multi-site physical restaurants.

The model will be focused on delivery partners and direct ordering/delivery.

## SBB Dashboard

SBB Dashboard was originally built to run multiple Smash Brothers Burgers stores.

It should now evolve into an all-in-one POS, analysis, and marketing machine.

The long-term plan is to offer it as a SaaS product, using Smash Brothers Burgers and related food brands as the test subjects and proving ground.

## Strategic importance

Mission Control and its agent team will become critical to connecting the different pieces of Cameron's business puzzle and scaling them.

The system must help coordinate ideas, execution, knowledge, agents, businesses, tools, and workflows into one operational command centre.

## Operating principle

Mission Control must be built for speed to market, autonomous execution, clear allocation, and practical outcomes.

The goal is not another dashboard full of information. The goal is a headquarters where work gets received, understood, allocated, executed, reported, remembered, and improved.
