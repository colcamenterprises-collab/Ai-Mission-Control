# Video Creative Production

Use this skill under Seth for approved marketing creative and product-demo work.

## Workflow

BRIEF → SOURCE → GENERATE/CAPTURE → EDIT → QA → EXPORT → REVIEW.

1. Start from an approved creative brief: objective, audience, message, proof, CTA, format, dimensions and acceptance criteria.
2. Prefer real product UI capture for product demonstrations. Generated footage must not fabricate product capability.
3. Use an available approved video backend rather than hard-coding a vendor. Text-to-video, image-to-video and reference-video are interchangeable capabilities selected by availability and brief fit.
4. Produce required aspect ratios/variants without creating a separate customer-facing video dashboard.
5. Visually inspect every output for malformed text, invented branding, factual mismatch, unsafe content, continuity errors and unusable crops.
6. Preserve generation/export metadata and evidence so Mission Control can reproduce and verify the asset.
7. Return drafts to Seth for marketing QA. External publication, paid activation and spend follow the existing approval policy.

## Mission Control intent

Video is a company Skill assigned to agents, not a standalone product surface. The owner should see completed assets and approval decisions, not model/provider complexity.
